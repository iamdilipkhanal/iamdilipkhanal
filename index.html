02 / Selected work

From material fabrication and experimentally calibrated device models to ANSYS package-scale thermo-mechanics and qualified global-to-local sensitivity studies.

W.01

2.5D chiplet–interposer package: global-to-local thermo-mechanical analysis

Ongoing independent ANSYS research. Built a seven-body idealized global package model and displacement-driven three-body local elastic replica. Under a single-variable substrate CTE change (13.2→17.0 ppm/K), the model predicted a 35.07% increase in the substrate-bottom out-of-plane displacement range and a 13.35% increase in the selected local effective-layer interior stress metric.

Controlled numerical sensitivity only: no discrete solder fatigue or physical validation claimed.

W.02

Frequency dispersion in ultrathin In₂O₃/HfO₂ MOS capacitors

At National Taiwan University, I built and calibrated Silvaco Victory Device models against measured C–V and Gp/ω data. The study showed that distributed bulk traps reproduced the observed frequency dispersion more convincingly than an interface-only explanation.

W.03

Graphene-reinforced PUA for stereolithographic 3D printing

I worked across material preparation, SLA fabrication, mechanical and electrical testing, and characterization. Higher conductivity came with a real trade-off: graphene agglomeration reduced tensile and impact performance.

W.04

Hydrothermal ZnO nanowires for UV sensing

I prepared seeded substrates, grew ZnO nanowires, and used FESEM and EDAX for characterization. Sparse nanowire coverage and substrate-dominated signals exposed the gap between a synthesis result and a reliable device.
